# Attribution

## The method

**Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH)**, devised by Richards J. Heuer, Jr.:

> Richards J. Heuer, Jr., *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, Center for the Study of
> Intelligence, Central Intelligence Agency, 1999 — chapter 8.
> https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf
> (U.S. federal work, public domain)

Second, independent statement of the procedure, used as a cross-check:

> *A Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic Techniques for Improving Intelligence Analysis*,
> U.S. Government, March 2009 — Analysis of Competing Hypotheses; Key Assumptions Check.
> https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-PREX3-PURL-gpo587/pdf/GOVPUB-PREX3-PURL-gpo587.pdf
> (U.S. federal work, public domain)

Heuer later developed the method further with Randolph H. Pherson in *Structured Analytic
Techniques for Intelligence Analysis* (CQ Press, 2011), which is in copyright and has **not**
been used in preparing this module; nothing here is drawn from it.

Open-source implementations of ACH exist, including the PARC "ACH" tool. This module is an
independent implementation from the public-domain texts above.
